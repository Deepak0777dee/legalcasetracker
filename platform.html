Document Intelligence

Analyze Thousands of Documents in Seconds

Our NLP-powered engine reads, categorizes, and extracts key information from legal documents at machine speed with human-level understanding.

Upload & Classify

Drag and drop documents in any format — PDFs, Word, scanned images. Our OCR and classification engine automatically categorizes each document by type, relevance, and case association.

Extract & Index

Key clauses, dates, parties, monetary amounts, and obligations are automatically extracted and indexed. Full-text search across your entire document library with instant results.

Analyze & Compare

Cross-reference documents against precedents and identify inconsistencies, hidden risks, and favorable clauses. Side-by-side comparison tools highlight critical differences.

Report & Present

Generate executive summaries, annotated briefs, and evidence matrices automatically. Export in court-ready formats with proper citation formatting and exhibit numbering.

Risk Intelligence

Quantify Every Case's Risk Profile

Make informed decisions with our proprietary risk scoring engine that evaluates 50+ factors to give you a clear picture of every case's risk landscape.

Litigation Risk Score

Each case receives a composite risk score from 0-100 based on jurisdiction volatility, case complexity, evidence strength, and historical win rates for similar matters. Updated in real-time as new information surfaces.

Financial Exposure Analysis

Calculate potential financial exposure including damages, legal fees, opportunity costs, and settlement ranges. Our models factor in inflation, interest rates, and jurisdiction-specific damage caps for accurate projections.

Compliance Monitoring

Track regulatory compliance across all active matters with automated alerts for changing regulations, filing requirements, and ethical obligations. Stay ahead of compliance risks before they become issues.
